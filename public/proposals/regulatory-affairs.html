01 / 05 — Your starting point

Complete scope of regulatory and scientific services

In a context of increasingly stringent regulatory requirements in Europe and globally, PHAGECON provides specialised Regulatory Affairs services. From initial strategic guidance to dossier preparation, Marketing Authorisation (MA) submission and post-authorisation management, we tailor our approach to each project. Our critical, forward-looking assessment helps simplify processes, reduce delays and minimise the risk of Lists of Questions (LoQs).
